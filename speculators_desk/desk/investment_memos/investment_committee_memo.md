# Investment Committee Memorandum

To: Investment Committee   From: ________   Date: ________   Re: ________

1. **Recommendation** (buy / sell / hold / hedge / wait / pass), amount, and terms.
2. **Thesis and what is priced.**
3. **Valuation**: method, key assumptions with sources, sensitivity table.
4. **Capital structure and liquidity**: sources and uses, debt terms, reserves, maturity.
5. **Scenarios and downside**: probabilities, outcomes, survival analysis.
6. **Risks**: ranked, with mitigants; which cannot be hedged.
7. **Why now; why this rather than Treasuries.**
8. **Invalidation and exit plan.**
9. **Counterparty analysis**: what forces the other side to transact.
10. **Open diligence items and walk-away conditions.**

Appendix: model outputs, data sources with grades, comparable evidence.
