# Reasoning-Quality Rubric (open-ended problems, drills, and IC answers)

Score each dimension 0–4. The overall score is the mean. Mastery requires a mean of at least 3.0 and no 0 on dimensions 4 or 5.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mechanism | No causal story | Plausible story, untested | Explicit chain from driver to cash flow, discount rate, financing, or positioning, with magnitudes |
| 2 | What is priced | Ignored | Mentions consensus | Identifies the market-implied expectation and how the view differs |
| 3 | Quantification | None | Partial numbers | Key numbers computed, with sensitivity to the most important input |
| 4 | Risk and invalidation | Absent | Vague ("if it goes wrong") | Observable, dated invalidation; maximum loss; survival check |
| 5 | Sizing and liquidity | Absent | Mentioned | Size tied to loss tolerance, correlation, and financing or margin |
| 6 | Alternatives | One option only | Lists alternatives | Compares with doing nothing, Treasuries, and at least one other use of capital |
| 7 | Evidence discipline | Narrative only | Some sources | Facts, inferences, and assumptions labeled; source quality noted |
| 8 | Intellectual honesty | Overconfident | Notes uncertainty | States the strongest competing view and what would change the decision |

A recommendation to wait or to pass can score 4 on every dimension.
